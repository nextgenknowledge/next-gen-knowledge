Everything on this site, including our courses, videos, quizzes, certificates and mini-games, is free. We don't sell anything, and there are no accounts, subscriptions or paid plans.

Donations are optional and go to NextGen Knowledge, a fiscally sponsored project of the Hack Club Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. They are processed on Hack Club's donation form, which is shown on our donate page.

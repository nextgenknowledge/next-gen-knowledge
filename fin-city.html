/// More than a game

Welcome to a new kind of classroom.

Fin City is being developed to turn financial concepts into interactive experiences. Instead of just reading about money, players can explore a virtual environment and learn through decisions, activities, and experimentation.

01 / EXPLORE

A City to Discover

Navigate a virtual city and discover different locations. Each destination introduces a different part of the financial world and gives players new opportunities to learn.

→ FIND YOUR WAY
02 / MANAGE

Money in Your Hands

Practice working with virtual money, think through financial decisions, and learn how spending, saving, and planning can influence your goals.

→ MAKE IT COUNT
03 / INVEST

Discover Investing

Get introduced to investing concepts and market decisions through virtual activities designed to make complex financial ideas easier to understand.

→ THINK LONG TERM
04 / LEARN

Learn by Doing

Connect financial concepts to interactive activities so learning becomes something you experience rather than something you only memorize.

→ PUT KNOWLEDGE TO WORK
05 / PROGRESS

Build Your Skills

Work toward learning goals and build your understanding of essential financial concepts as you progress through the experience.

→ KEEP LEVELING UP
06 / PREPARE

Prepare for Real Life

Build familiarity with financial topics that matter beyond the screen, from managing money to understanding how everyday financial decisions work.

→ SKILLS FOR THE FUTURE
/// Your adventure awaits

Every decision moves you forward.

The idea behind Fin City is simple: financial literacy should feel relevant, engaging, and useful. By putting players in a virtual world, we want to make it easier to experiment with financial ideas and connect what they learn to real life.

Fin City is an upcoming project from NextGen Knowledge, built around the belief that practical education should be accessible to the next generation.

01

Enter the World

Start your journey in a virtual city built around real-world financial concepts.

02

Explore and Experiment

Visit locations, interact with financial systems, and learn by making choices in a virtual environment.

03

Build Financial Knowledge

Develop a stronger understanding of money management, banking, investing, and financial decision-making.

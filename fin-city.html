The world

Five cities. Five money skills.

Every city has its own streets, weather and buildings, and each one teaches a different part of money. Step through a portal in Fin-City Park to travel.

EARNING, CAREERS & TECH

San Francisco

Get a job, negotiate a salary, file a tax return and spot online scams.

INVESTING & MARKETS

New York

Buy and sell stocks, bonds and index funds in a market that moves.

BUDGETING & SMART SHOPPING

London

Plan a budget, take the grocery challenge and spot whether a deal is really a deal.

SAVING, PROTECTION & THE LONG TERM

Features

Learn money by living it.

40+ kinds of activities

Walk into a building and play. Every activity is built around a real money skill, from sorting needs and wants to filing taxes and pricing a business.

  • Quests, challenges and seasonal events
  • Earn up to 3 stars on every run
  • A quick lesson before each activity, with help when you need it

Invest in a market that moves

Buy and sell shares in fictional companies, index funds and bonds. Prices rise and fall over time, so you see why spreading out your money matters.

  • Stocks, index funds and bonds
  • Risk and dividends explained in plain words
  • Your net worth updates as you play

Your guide, NextGen Narwhal

NextGen Narwhal walks with you, explains what to do next and cheers you on. Tap the narwhal any time you get stuck.

  • Read-aloud for younger players
  • Bank, goals, investing and quests one tap away
  • A FinPhone with apps for your money

Find your way around

Open the city map, pick a building and follow the gold trail, or jump straight there. Ride bikes and cars once you've saved up for them.

Two levels, one game

Entry (ages 6–13) has big buttons, pictures and lots of hints. Advanced (ages 13–18) uses real calculations: compound interest, credit scores, taxes, P/E ratios and business math. Switch any time.

Also included

Made for players, parents and teachers.

No account needed

No sign-up and no ads. Progress saves in your browser, and you can export it as a file.

Progress report

Download a teacher and parent report right from the pause menu.

Runs on Chromebooks

Low, Medium and High quality settings keep it smooth on school laptops.

Make it yours

Create your character, earn outfits from quests, own a home and adopt a pet.

Learn hub

Brush up on any topic with short lessons and a glossary of money words.

Nicknames only

Players use a fun nickname, never a real name. We use Google Analytics to count visits and milestones.

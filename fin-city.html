What's inside

A new kind of classroom.

Instead of reading about money, players learn by making decisions and seeing what happens.

01 / TWO MODESACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED

Made for 6 to 18

Entry (ages 6–13) has big buttons, read-aloud and generous hints. Advanced (13–18) uses real calculations, taxes, credit scores and markets.

02 / PLAYACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED

40+ kinds of activities

Step inside buildings across five cities and take on activities, quests and challenges, each one built around a real money skill.

03 / LIVE ITACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED

Your own financial life

Run a simulated life of your own: earn, budget, save and invest, and watch how today's choices shape tomorrow.

04 / LEVEL UPACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED

Quests, outfits & the FinPhone

Complete quests to earn outfits, use the apps on your FinPhone, and brush up anytime in the Learn hub.

05 / PRIVATEACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED

Private by design

No accounts, ads or tracking. Your progress saves in your own browser, and you can export it as a file.

06 / FOR CLASSROOMSACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED

Teacher & parent report

Download a progress report right from the pause menu. It runs well on Chromebooks too, with Low, Medium and High quality settings.

How to play

Three steps to your first city.

1
STEP 1

Go to fincity.dev

It runs right in your browser. Nothing to download or sign up for.

2
STEP 2

Make a world

Pick a nickname (never your real name) and create your world.

3
STEP 3

Choose your mode

Go Entry or Advanced, then start exploring from Fin-City Park.

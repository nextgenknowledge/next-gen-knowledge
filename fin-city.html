Welcome to a new kind of classroom.

Your adventure awaits

Every decision moves you forward.

The idea behind Fin City is simple: financial literacy should feel relevant, engaging, and useful. By putting players in a virtual world, we want to make it easier to experiment with financial ideas and connect what they learn to real life.

Fin City is an upcoming project from NextGen Knowledge, built around the belief that practical education should be accessible to the next generation.

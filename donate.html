/// Our Mission

Help Students Build Real-World Skills

NextGen Knowledge is a student-led financial literacy organization focused on making practical education accessible to the next generation.

Financial Literacy

Help students learn about budgeting, banking, investing, and other essential financial concepts.

Accessible Education

Support free learning opportunities and educational programs for students.

Community Outreach

Help bring practical financial education to more schools, libraries, and community organizations.

Future Learning Projects

Help us develop new educational experiences that make real-world skills engaging and accessible.

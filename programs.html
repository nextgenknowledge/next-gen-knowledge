The Next-Gen Method

We don't stop at teaching.

Students repeatedly move from a concept to a decision, then see the consequences of that decision in a simulated environment.

Concept → Decision → Feedback

Every stage is designed to make financial concepts tangible.

01

Learn

Understand the concept through clear instruction and discussion.

02

Simulate

Test ideas through budgets, portfolios, scenarios, and challenges.

03

Analyze

Evaluate results, identify tradeoffs, and understand why outcomes changed.

04

Apply

Present ideas, defend decisions, and connect lessons to the real world.

The Advanced Curriculum

Ten classes. Five themes.

Students progress from understanding companies to building portfolios and ultimately defending their own investment thesis.

Week 01 01

Valuation & Financial Statements

Class 1

How Investors Think — intrinsic value, Mr. Market, safety margins, and company comparisons.

Class 2

Financial Statements Made Simple — revenue, profit, balance sheets, and company analysis.

Week 02 02

Moats & Investor Psychology

Class 3

Billion Dollar Companies — economic moats, network effects, and business case studies.

Class 4

Psychology of Investing — FOMO, bubbles, behavioral finance, and historical market crises.

Week 03 03

Portfolios & Business Models

Class 5

Building Portfolios — diversification, risk tolerance, and launching the $100K virtual portfolio.

Class 6

Business Models — subscription, marketplace, franchise, advertising, and luxury models.

Week 04 04

Buffett Metrics & Entrepreneurship

Class 7

Reading Companies Like Warren Buffett — ROE, margins, free cash flow, and company analysis.

Class 8

Entrepreneurship & Unit Economics — scaling, recurring revenue, CAC, and LTV.

Week 05 FINAL SHOWCASE

Mistakes & Investment Competition

Class 9

Biggest Investing Mistakes — emotional investing, market timing, and concentration risk.

Class 10

Investment Competition — students present a company thesis, financials, and risks.

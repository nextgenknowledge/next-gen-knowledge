The Money Masters Curriculum

A high-impact, 5-week interactive program built explicitly for middle schoolers (Grades 5–8) to master modern wealth building, budgeting, and entrepreneurial thinking.

Track S&P 500, Nasdaq, Bitcoin, gold, and student-led news presentations.

Main Lesson

Complete 5-Week Curriculum

Think like investors, analyze financial statements, and build companies.

Week 1

Valuation & Financial Statements

  • Class 1: How Investors Think (Intrinsic Value, Mr. Market, Safety Margins, Apple vs Crocs vs Costco).
  • Class 2: Financial Statements Made Simple (Revenue, Profit, Balance Sheets, Analyzing Nike).
Week 2

Moats & Investor Psychology

  • Class 3: Billion Dollar Companies (Economic Moats, Network Effects, Case Studies like Visa & Amazon).
  • Class 4: Psychology of Investing (FOMO, Bubbles, Behavioral Finance, Tulip Mania, Dot-Com Crisis).
Week 3

Portfolios & Business Models

  • Class 5: Building Portfolios (Diversification, Risk Tolerance, Launching the $100k Virtual Portfolio).
  • Class 6: Business Models (Subscription, Marketplace, Franchise, Advertising, Luxury models).
Week 4

Buffett Metrics & Entrepreneurship

  • Class 7: Reading Companies Like Warren Buffett (ROE, Margins, Free Cash Flow, Disney & Tesla).
  • Class 8: Entrepreneurship & Unit Economics (Scaling, Recurring Revenue, CAC, LTV, Lemonade Stand Redesign).
Week 5

Mistakes & Final Investment Competition

  • Class 9: Biggest Investing Mistakes (Emotional Investing, Timing the Market, Concentration Risk).
  • Class 10: Investment Competition (Students present their favorite company thesis, financials, and risks. Class votes & awards).

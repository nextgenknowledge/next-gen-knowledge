Our approach

Learn it. Practice it. Put it to work.

Financial literacy becomes more meaningful when students can connect concepts to decisions. Our programs pair instruction with hands-on practice to build understanding and confidence.

STAGE 01

Learn

Understand key concepts through structured lessons and guided discussion.

STAGE 02

Simulate

Work through realistic scenarios and practice making informed decisions.

STAGE 03

Analyze

Compare alternatives, evaluate trade-offs, and explain your reasoning.

STAGE 04

Apply

Use your knowledge in projects, financial plans, and investment challenges.

Investor Academy curriculum

10 classes · 5 weeks
Week · ClassesTopic
WK1 · 1–2Markets and Business ModelsExplore financial markets, how businesses generate revenue, and the role of competitive advantage.
WK2 · 3–4Financial StatementsRead the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement; examine important financial metrics.
WK3 · 5–6Valuation and Investor ThinkingExplore valuation fundamentals, business quality, competitive moats, behavioral finance, and common investing mistakes.
WK4 · 7–8Portfolios and Investment ResearchBuild a virtual portfolio, research companies, evaluate risks, and develop a structured investment thesis.
WK5 · 9Thesis PreparationRefine company research, review financials, identify key risks, and prepare a persuasive investment presentation.
WK5 · 10Investment CompetitionPresent a company thesis, discuss financials and risks, answer questions, participate in voting, and recognize outstanding work with awards.
ABOUT THE $100K PORTFOLIO The $100K portfolio is a virtual educational simulation, not real money or a promise of investment returns. Activities are designed to teach research, risk awareness, and analytical thinking.

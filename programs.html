Our programs

Two paths. One stronger foundation.

Whether you're learning the basics of personal finance or ready to analyze investments, there's a place to start building your skills.

FOUNDATIONAL PROGRAM

Money Masters

Develop the financial skills to make thoughtful decisions about spending, saving, investing, and entrepreneurship.

Grades 5–8 5 weeks 10 live classes

What you'll learn

  • Spending and budgeting

    Understand needs, wants, trade-offs, and how to plan around limited resources.

  • Saving and financial planning

    Set goals, prepare for future expenses, and evaluate financial choices.

  • Investing fundamentals

    Explore stocks, risk, returns, diversification, and long-term thinking.

  • Entrepreneurship

    Explore business ideas, costs, revenue, and how businesses create value.

The experience

Interactive lessons, practical scenarios, guided activities, and opportunities to apply concepts to everyday life.

ADVANCED PROGRAM

Investor Academy

Grades 7+ 5 weeks 10 live classes

What you'll explore

  • Business and company analysis

  • Portfolio construction

    Explore diversification, portfolio decisions, investment risks, and market behavior.

Our approach

Learn it. Practice it. Put it to work.

Financial literacy becomes more meaningful when students can connect concepts to decisions. Our programs pair instruction with hands-on practice to build understanding and confidence.

Inside the programs

A structured path from concepts to application.

Explore the main topics covered throughout each five-week program.

Program 01

Money Masters curriculum

Foundational financial literacy
Week 01

Money Fundamentals

Money, earning, spending choices, needs, wants, and opportunity cost.

Week 02

Budgeting and Saving

Budgets, financial goals, saving strategies, and planning ahead.

Week 03

Investing Basics

Stocks, risk and return, diversification, and compound growth.

Week 04

Entrepreneurship

Business ideas, customers, costs, revenue, and creating value.

Week 05

Putting It Together

Apply financial concepts to integrated scenarios and planning challenges.

Program 02

Investor Academy curriculum

10 classes · 5 weeks
01

Week 1 · Classes 1–2

Markets and Business Models

Explore financial markets, how businesses generate revenue, and the role of competitive advantage.

02

Week 2 · Classes 3–4

Financial Statements

Read the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement; examine important financial metrics.

03

Week 3 · Classes 5–6

Valuation and Investor Thinking

Explore valuation fundamentals, business quality, competitive moats, behavioral finance, and common investing mistakes.

04

Week 4 · Classes 7–8

Portfolios and Investment Research

Build a virtual portfolio, research companies, evaluate risks, and develop a structured investment thesis.

05

Week 5 · Class 9

Thesis Preparation

Refine company research, review financials, identify key risks, and prepare a persuasive investment presentation.

Week 5 · Class 10

Investment Competition

Present a company thesis, discuss financials and risks, answer questions, participate in voting, and recognize outstanding work with awards.

The $100K portfolio is a virtual educational simulation, not real money or a promise of investment returns. Activities are designed to teach research, risk awareness, and analytical thinking.

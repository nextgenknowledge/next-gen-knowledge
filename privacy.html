The fine print, in plain words

Privacy policy.

NextGen Knowledge is a student-led organization. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell your information.

Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy covers nextgenknowledge.org. Our game, Fin City, has its own short policy at fincity.dev/privacy.

No accounts

You don't need an account to use this site, and we don't ask for your email address, phone number, school or address anywhere on it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and course lessons people use, so we can improve them. It loads only after you scroll, tap or type on a page. Google Analytics uses cookies and sees basic device information like browser type and rough location. Course progress events never include your name. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or an analytics blocker and the site works the same.

The feedback form

The Fin City page has a feedback form. When you send it, the topic you pick, your feedback and the name you type (or "Anonymous" if you leave it blank) go to a Google Form run by our team. Please don't include personal details like your full name, school or contact information.

Free courses and games

Your course progress, quiz results, the name you type on your certificate and mini-game scores are saved only in your own browser. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Videos

Course videos are hosted on YouTube and shown in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may store information on your device once you play a video, under Google's privacy policy.

Donations

The donation form is provided by Hack Club, our fiscal sponsor, and runs on Hack Club's own site inside our page. Your payment details go to Hack Club and its payment processor, not to us, and Hack Club's privacy policy covers that form. For questions about a donation, including canceling a monthly gift or asking about a refund, email us.

Fonts

Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to fetch them.

Who we share information with

We don't sell or rent information, and we don't share it for advertising. The services named above (Google Analytics, Google Forms, YouTube, Google Fonts and Hack Club) and our host, GitHub Pages, process data for us to run the site.

Children

Our programs are made for students, including children under 13. Because the site has no accounts and doesn't ask for personal information, students can use it without telling us who they are. If you're a parent or teacher and think a child sent us personal information through the feedback form, email us and we'll delete it.

Not financial advice

Everything on this site, in our courses and in Fin City is for education only. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Examples and simulated returns are for learning, and past performance does not guarantee future results.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions or deletion requests can go to nextgen.knowledge123@gmail.com.

Learning for everyone

Accessibility statement.

Our courses and site are free for every student, and that includes students with disabilities.

Last updated November 15, 2026

We want every student to be able to use our site and courses. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and we keep fixing the places that don't meet it yet. Our game, Fin City, has its own statement at fincity.dev/accessibility.

What we do

  • Every page works with a keyboard, and the item you're on is shown with a clear gold outline.
  • Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labels so screen readers can move around them.
  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative ones are hidden from screen readers.
  • If your device asks for less motion, the moving ticker and other animations stop.
  • Text can be zoomed to 200% and pages fit phone screens without sideways scrolling.
  • Course videos play on YouTube, where you can turn on captions.

Known limitations

Some of the mini-games are timed or need quick mouse or touch input, and some older images may still be missing a good description. We're working through them.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site is hard or impossible for you to use, email nextgen.knowledge123@gmail.com and tell us which page it was and what device and browser you were using. We aim to reply within 5 business days.

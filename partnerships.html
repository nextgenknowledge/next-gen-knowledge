Meet our partners.

We work alongside organizations that are creating new ways for students to learn, connect, and prepare for their futures.

Bridge Finance Network

Finance educationEstablished

Bridge Finance Network is a 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to equipping students with valuable skills and insights into the finance industry. They prioritize networking and building authentic connections for passionate, ambitious students. Their goal is to provide a realistic perspective of the financial world, helping students become the next generation of global financial leaders.

Moniqo

Financial literacyComing soon

A student-led nonprofit breaking barriers to financial education and career success, free for everyone in Queens and Brooklyn. They teach budgeting, investing, credit, resume building, and interview skills, hands-on and at no cost.

Lumera

AI & personal financeComing soon

Lumera is an AI-powered personal finance platform built to make financial intelligence simple, accessible, and practical for young people. Ways to work together

From introduction to impact.

We keep the process straightforward so we can focus on shared goals and practical next steps. We welcome conversations about partnerships that align with our mission and create value for students:

  • Educational programs. Workshops, lessons, and student learning experiences.
  • Mentorship & speakers. Useful perspectives and real-world experience.
  • Funding & resources. Sponsorships, donations, and resources that support our programs.
  • Community outreach. Reach more students through shared outreach efforts.
  1. 01

    Start a conversation

    Tell us about your organization, its mission, and what you hope to accomplish together.

  2. 02

    Identify shared goals

    Explore how your expertise, resources, or programs could align with Next-Gen Knowledge's educational mission.

  3. 03

    Plan the collaboration

    Discuss practical next steps, responsibilities, and how the collaboration could serve students.

Finance Education Established Partner

Bridge Finance Network

Bridge Finance Network is a 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to equipping students with valuable skills and insights into the finance industry. They prioritize networking and building authentic connections for passionate, ambitious students. Their goal is to provide a realistic perspective of the financial world, helping students become the next generation of global financial leaders.

Moniqo

A student-led nonprofit breaking barriers to financial education and career success, free for everyone in Queens and Brooklyn. They teach budgeting, investing, credit, resume building, and interview skills—hands on at no cost.

Lumera

Lumera is an AI-powered personal finance platform built to make financial intelligence simple, accessible, and practical for young people. From financial education and market insights to career and university guidance, Lumera brings the tools and knowledge you need to make smarter financial decisions, all in one place.

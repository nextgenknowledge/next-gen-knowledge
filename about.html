Our story

It started with a simple question.

What if students could learn important life skills before they needed them?

That question helped inspire a student-led effort to make practical education more accessible, engaging, and relevant to young learners.

Quick answers

Before you begin your quest.

A few things to know about NextGen Knowledge.

What is NextGen Knowledge?

NextGen Knowledge is a student-led educational initiative focused on making practical financial literacy and real-world learning accessible to the next generation.

Who are the programs designed for?

Our programs are designed for young learners. Age and grade requirements vary by program, so visit the Programs page for current details.

Do I need experience to get started?

You don't need to know everything before you begin. Check the details of each program to find an option that fits your interests and experience.

How can I get involved?

Students and families can explore our programs, and schools or community organizations can visit our Partnerships page to learn about opportunities to work together.

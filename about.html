Our mission

Make real-world education accessible.

NextGen Knowledge is a student-led educational organization focused on making practical learning available to the next generation.

We believe students should have opportunities to explore financial concepts, ask questions, practice decision-making, and develop skills that can support them throughout life. We aim to make that learning approachable through free educational programs, community workshops, and new interactive experiences.

What guides us

Our work starts with what matters.

These principles shape how we design our programs, work with communities, and approach educational opportunities.

Learning should be within reach.

We work to lower barriers to educational opportunities. Our free programs and outreach efforts aim to help more students access useful knowledge, regardless of their previous experience.

Knowledge should have a purpose.

We emphasize concepts students can apply outside a lesson. Through examples, activities, and guided exercises, we aim to connect learning with real decisions and everyday life.

Progress happens together.

Students, families, volunteers, educators, and community organizations all play a role in expanding access to education. We value collaboration and shared learning.

There is always a better way to learn.

We explore new formats for educational experiences, including interactive projects, simulations, and technology-enabled tools that can make learning more engaging.

The fine print

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Learn more about our organization, our educational programs, and ways to get involved.

Ask us a question →
Who can take part?
Anyone. Our live courses (Money Masters and Investor Academy) are paused right now, but Fin City is open to everyone ages 6–18 at fincity.dev. Schools and community groups can also partner with us on workshops.
Do students have to pay to participate?
No. Everything we offer is free, including Fin City.
What is Fin City?
Fin City is our free 3D financial-literacy game for ages 6–18, now in beta. Players explore five cities, play 40+ kinds of activities and run a simulated financial life. Learn more or play at fincity.dev.
Can a school, library, or community center partner with us?
Yes. We welcome conversations with schools, libraries, community centers, and other organizations interested in financial literacy workshops or educational partnerships. Visit our Partnerships page to learn more.
How can I support NextGen Knowledge?
You can explore our programs, share our educational resources, discuss a community partnership, contribute to our work, or learn about opportunities to join the team. Visit our Donate page or Team page to get started.

A whole city is loading, and it runs on money skills. Fin City is NextGen Knowledge's free financial-literacy game: walk the streets, step into the bank, budget your way through the marketplace, take your first shot at investing, and watch your choices build your future. No textbook. No lecture. Just you, a starting balance, and a city to figure out.

  • Make real decisions. Spend, save, and invest virtual money and see what happens.
  • Level up. Build skills that carry over to real life.

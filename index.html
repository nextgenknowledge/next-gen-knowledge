The Educational Gap

Financial literacy shouldn't start after graduation.

Students encounter money every day, yet practical financial concepts are often left outside the classroom. We believe students should have the opportunity to understand money before they are responsible for managing it.

Start early

Build healthy habits around saving, spending, goal setting, and digital banking.

Learn from peers

High school instructors make complex concepts relatable and relevant.

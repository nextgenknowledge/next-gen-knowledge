                The Educational Gap                 

Why Financial Literacy Cannot Wait Until Adulthood

                

                    Traditional school curriculums rarely teach students how money actually works in the real world. By introducing financial concepts between 5th and 8th grade, students build healthy habits regarding saving, smart consumer choices, and long-term planning before facing real economic decisions.                 

                
                    
                        
✓
                        

Early Habits: Decisions made early build confidence with digital banking, budgeting apps, and goal setting.

                    
                    
                        
✓
                        

Peer Mentorship: High school instructors make economic principles relatable, engaging, and genuinely fun.

Our flagship project

A new world for money skills.

Fin City is an interactive financial-literacy game being developed by NextGen Knowledge. The idea is simple: make financial concepts easier to understand by letting students explore a virtual world and learn through decisions.

  • Explore a virtual city. Discover locations with different financial purposes.
  • Practice money management. Learn about budgeting, banking, and investing.
  • Learn through gameplay. Build familiarity with real-world financial decisions.

Fin City is in development. Features and availability may change as the project evolves.
